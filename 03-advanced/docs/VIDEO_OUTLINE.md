# Video outline (3 to 5 minutes)

The task list asks for one self-recorded video, 3 to 5 minutes, shared through a Google
Drive link in the submission form. Use your own words. Record the screen and talk over it.
Aim for about 4 minutes and do one practice run first.

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:20 | GitHub repo front page | Your name and the three levels. One file structure with a folder for each level |
| 0:20 to 1:05 | Level 1: Excel dashboard | Superstore data; what was wrong with the raw file and how you cleaned it; the headline KPIs; one finding (discounts above 20% lose money) |
| 1:05 to 2:00 | Level 2: report and charts | Online Retail data; why customer-level analysis; repeat buyers produce most revenue; retention and RFM; two recommendations |
| 2:00 to 3:50 | Level 3: Power BI dashboard | IBM HR data; what you transformed; why these KPIs; click through the three pages, use a slicer, the decomposition tree and drill-through; the overtime finding; the watchlist |
| 3:50 to 4:20 | Recommendations | Two or three actions and why |
| 4:20 to 4:40 | Limits | Fictional data, association not cause, what you would do next |

Practise saying these four things clearly: **how you cleaned the data, why you chose the
KPIs, how the dashboard is organised, and what a manager should do because of it.**
